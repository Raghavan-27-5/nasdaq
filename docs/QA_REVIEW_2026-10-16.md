# QA Review — Pipeline & Dashboard Bug Audit (2026-10-16)

**Scope.** Full read of the acquisition/derivation pipeline (`build_notebook.py` cells 2–9),
the fixture generator (`gen_demo_data.py`), the harness (`dry_run_notebook.py`), the frontend
data layer and all eight pages; plus an independent recomputation of every emitted metric from
the frozen raw Parquet (`artifacts/acquisition_v001/prices_v001.parquet`).

**Method.** No candidate finding was accepted or dismissed by inspection alone. Each was
recomputed numerically from the stored acquisition and compared against the emitted bundle
before classification as *defect*, *correct*, or *false alarm*. All confirmed defects are fixed;
regression checks were added so none can silently return.

**Verdict.** Six confirmed defects (one high-severity content bug, one high-severity reporting
bug, four moderate/low), all fixed and shipped via a reprocess of the frozen acquisition.
The remaining large majority of the pipeline verified exact against recomputation.

## 1. Confirmed defects (fixed)

### B1 — Factor z-rank sign inversion (HIGH) — `build_notebook.py` `build_fund_rows()`

**Symptom.** `zrank(vals, higher_better)` mapped the *best* value per its own flag to −1 while
the factor weight also carried the direction — two sign conventions contradicting each other.
Net effect: **momentum, drawdown and diversification were silently inverted** in the AI-Universe
composite (volatility, with a negative weight, accidentally survived).

**Evidence (emitted bundle vs Parquet recomputation).**

| Factor | Highest raw value | Contribution emitted | Correct sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| momentum | MU +460% (12-1) | **−0.40** (should be +0.40) | inverted |
| drawdown | AMD 0.0% (at 52w high) | **−0.15** (should be +0.15) | inverted |
| diversification | NVDA ρ̄ 0.44 (worst diversifier) | **+0.25** (should be −0.25) | inverted |
| volatility | CRWV 102% | −0.20 | correct |

**Fix.** Signed rank in [−1, +1] with +1 = highest raw value; direction carried **only** by the
weight sign (`diversification` weight corrected +0.25 → −0.25 in the real set; `drawdown` and
`diversification` signs corrected in the fixture set). Composite ranks changed materially —
new top-5: PANW, GOOGL, AMD, DDOG, MU (old: CDNS, AVGO, SNPS, ADBE, NVDA).

**Regression cover.** Five harness checks (orientation of all four factors + composite identity).

### B2 — `momentum_12_1` was plain 12-month momentum (MEDIUM) — `build_fund_rows()`

The field name and the factor definition said 12-1 (Jegadeesh–Titman, skip the most recent
month); the code computed `P_t / P_{t−252} − 1` with no skip. NVDA: emitted 0.2986 vs true
12-1 0.1816. Fixed to `P_{t−20} / P_{t−252} − 1` in notebook **and** fixture generator.

### B3 — "Sharpe" was CAGR ÷ vol, not a Sharpe ratio (MEDIUM) — `perf()` (×3 surfaces)

`sharpe = cagr / vol` overstates in high-vol series: EW universe 0.999 vs the standard
annualised mean ÷ std (rf = 0) of **0.860**. Present in the notebook, the fixture generator and
`StockIntelligence.tsx` (client-side per-name stats, labeled “Sharpe (rf 0)”). All three fixed
to annualised mean ÷ annualised vol; Sortino fixed the same way.

### B4 — `worst_30d` was a rolling MEAN, understating the worst month ~15× (HIGH, reporting)

The metric took `min()` of a 21-day rolling *mean* of daily log returns: EW −0.0197 displayed as
“Worst 30d −2.0%” when the actual worst 30-trading-day cumulative return in the window is
**−30.6%**. Fixed to the minimum 30-trading-day cumulative return (notebook + fixtures).
Note the fixture generator already used a cumulative convention (21d) — the two pipelines
disagreed; both are now 30d cumulative.

### B5 — Client-side CAGR annualised over the nominal range (LOW-MEDIUM) — `StockIntelligence.tsx`

For truncated names (CRWV: 1.5y of history), selecting “5Y” annualised over 5 years.
Fixed to annualise over the observed window `(n−1)/252`.

### B6 — Chart title “Growth of 100” vs rebased-to-1.00 values (COSMETIC) — `Backtest.tsx`

Charts rebase each series to its value at the OOS start (= 1.00). Title corrected to
“Rebased to 1.00 · out-of-sample”.

## 4. How the fixes were shipped

The corrected analytics were recomputed **from the frozen acquisition**, not re-fetched: new
harness mode `dry_run_notebook.py --from-parquet` rebuilds the frames from
`artifacts/acquisition_v001/prices_v001.parquet` and re-runs the full notebook processing chain.
Reprocess audit: **119/119 checks green**. `market.json` and `risk.json` are byte-identical to
the pre-fix bundle (only the affected artifacts changed); Parquet content is logically identical
(container metadata regenerated). Because Yahoo re-adjusts historical prices as new dividends
accrue, a re-fetch would have silently changed the dataset; reprocessing keeps the audited bytes.

Provenance: `run_context` is now recorded in `manifest.json` (acquisition block) and
`run_manifest.json` for every execution path (colab / live / reprocess / dry).
