# UNIVERSE_METHODOLOGY.md — Universe Construction Logic

## Purpose

Define exactly what the project means by a “top Nasdaq AI stock” before any factor ranking is performed.

## Important distinction

The project has two stages:

### Stage A — Structural universe screen

Construct a 20-name research universe using:

1. Nasdaq listing status;
2. US domicile under the project definition;
3. material AI economic exposure;
4. sufficient public financial disclosure;
5. sufficient market-data history for the assignment;
6. sufficient liquidity for later capacity testing;
7. coverage across multiple AI value-chain layers.

This stage is a **universe construction step**, not a claim that the 20 names are objectively the 20 best AI stocks in the market.

### Stage B — Quantitative ranking

Rank the locked 20 using the specified factors:

- AI revenue exposure;
- revenue growth;
- FCF growth/quality;
- valuation;
- AI-capex exposure;
- earnings revisions;
- momentum;
- volatility;
- drawdown;
- correlation/diversification.

The final top 15–20 portfolio universe is determined from the documented scoring rule, not from the agent's subjective preference.

## Selection principle

Prefer broad economic representation over duplicated exposure to a single AI layer.

The candidate set therefore includes:

- compute;
- memory;
- networking/custom silicon;
- hyperscalers;
- enterprise AI;
- AI cloud;
- semiconductor equipment;
- EDA;
- cybersecurity;
- generative-AI software;
- edge AI.

## Exclusions

`SNOW` is excluded because it is NYSE-listed.

`ARM` is excluded because the current experiment defines the US universe as US-domiciled operating companies.

## No hidden ranking

No agent may describe the 20-name Stage-A universe as “the definitive top 20” until the Stage-B factor ranking has been executed and documented.
